## Frequently Asked Questions (For App Store)

### Q: Is CallWall a law firm?
**A**: No, CallWall is NOT a law firm and does NOT provide legal advice. We provide tools and templates for legal self-help. Always consult a licensed attorney for legal advice specific to your situation.

### Q: Does CallWall actually block calls?
**A**: CallWall helps you track and identify unwanted callers. For blocking, we integrate with your device's native call blocking features. We don't intercept calls directly.

### Q: How accurate is the AI transcription?
**A**: CallWall uses OpenAI Whisper, one of the most advanced speech recognition models available. While highly accurate, transcriptions may contain errors, especially with poor audio quality or accents.

### Q: Is my data secure?
**A**: Yes! We use end-to-end encryption, secure cloud storage with Supabase, and never sell your data to third parties. We're GDPR and CCPA compliant. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

### Q: Can I use CallWall's legal documents in court?
**A**: CallWall's templates are designed based on FDCPA requirements and legal best practices. However, we recommend consulting an attorney before using any legal document in court or for formal legal action.

### Q: What's the difference between Free and Premium?
**A**: Free tier includes basic voicemail recording, phone tracking, and 5 legal documents per month. Premium adds unlimited recording, AI transcription, advanced threat analysis, unlimited documents, and priority support.

### Q: Does CallWall work offline?
**A**: Yes! You can record voicemails and generate documents offline. When you reconnect, CallWall automatically syncs your data and processes any AI features.

### Q: What is the AI Voice Playground?
**A**: Our Business tier includes AI voice generation using OpenAI TTS. Practice your responses to debt collectors with realistic AI voices in different tones (Professional, Stern, Calm, Firm). It's like a rehearsal tool for difficult conversations.

### Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
**A**: Yes! Cancel anytime through the app or your App Store/Play Store subscriptions. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

### Q: Why does CallWall need microphone access?
**A**: CallWall needs microphone access to record voicemails for transcription and analysis. We never access your microphone without your explicit action (pressing "Record"). See our Privacy Policy for details.
