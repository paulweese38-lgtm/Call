# Privacy Policy for CallWall

**Last Updated: December 1, 2025**

## Introduction

CallWall ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application CallWall (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you create an account and use CallWall, we collect:

- **Account Information**: Email address, password (encrypted), display name
- **Authentication Data**: Biometric data (stored locally on your device only for Face ID/Touch ID)
- **Profile Information**: Subscription tier, subscription status, usage preferences

### 1.2 Content You Create

- **Voicemail Recordings**: Audio files you record or upload for transcription
- **Legal Documents**: Information you enter in legal document forms (debtor names, addresses, case details)
- **Phone Numbers**: Phone numbers you add to your tracking list
- **Voice Generations**: Text you submit for AI voice generation
- **Communications**: Messages you analyze for threat detection

### 1.3 Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data**: Features you use, session duration, interaction patterns
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version, unique device identifiers
- **Log Data**: IP address, access times, app crashes, performance metrics
- **Storage Data**: Amount of storage used for audio and documents

### 1.4 Information from Third Parties

We receive information from:
- **Stripe**: Payment status, subscription information (when you subscribe to Premium or Business tiers)
- **Apple/Google**: Purchase verification for in-app subscriptions

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Core Functionality

- **Voicemail Transcription**: Audio files are sent to OpenAI Whisper API for transcription
- **Threat Analysis**: Transcripts and communications are analyzed using Anthropic Claude AI for threat detection and FDCPA compliance checking
- **Voice Generation**: Text is processed by OpenAI TTS API to generate AI voices
- **Legal Documents**: Generate PDF documents based on your input
- **Phone Management**: Store and manage phone numbers you track or block
- **Authentication**: Verify your identity and maintain your account security

### 2.2 Service Improvement

- **Analytics**: Understand how users interact with the app to improve features
- **Bug Fixes**: Identify and resolve technical issues
- **Feature Development**: Develop new features based on usage patterns

### 2.3 Communication

- **Service Updates**: Notify you about app updates, new features, or changes
- **Subscription Management**: Send receipts, subscription renewal reminders, billing notifications
- **Support**: Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

### 2.4 Legal Compliance

- **Legal Obligations**: Comply with applicable laws and regulations
- **Safety**: Protect against fraud, abuse, or illegal activity
- **Rights Protection**: Enforce our Terms of Service and protect our legal rights

## 4. Data Retention

### 4.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

- **Active Accounts**: We retain your data while your account is active
- **Voicemail Audio**: Stored until you delete it or close your account
- **Legal Documents**: Stored until you delete them or close your account
- **Phone Numbers**: Stored until you remove them or close your account
- **Usage Data**: Retained for 90 days for analytics purposes

### 4.2 Account Deletion

When you delete your account:
- Personal information is deleted within 30 days
- Backups may retain data for up to 90 days
- Some data may be retained longer if required by law
- Financial records kept for 7 years for tax compliance

### 4.3 Data Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of specific data at any time by:
- Using the in-app deletion features
- Contacting support@callwall.app
- We will respond within 30 days

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption in Transit**: All data transmitted using TLS/SSL encryption
- **Encryption at Rest**: Sensitive data encrypted in database
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access, least privilege principle
- **Authentication**: Secure password hashing (bcrypt), optional biometric authentication
- **Monitoring**: Automated threat detection and security monitoring
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and vulnerability scanning

### 5.2 Your Responsibilities

To keep your account secure:
- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID)
- Do not share your account credentials
- Log out on shared devices
- Report suspicious activity immediately

### 5.3 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will:
- Notify you within 72 hours of discovery
- Inform you of what data was affected
- Explain steps we're taking to address the breach
- Provide recommendations to protect yourself

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 Rights Under GDPR (European Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Restriction**: Request limitation of processing your data
- **Objection**: Object to processing of your data for specific purposes
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent at any time

### 6.2 Rights Under CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

- **Know**: What personal information we collect and how we use it
- **Delete**: Request deletion of your personal information
- **Opt-Out**: Opt-out of sale of personal information (we do NOT sell your data)
- **Non-Discrimination**: Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

### 6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights:
- **Email**: privacy@callwall.app
- **In-App**: Settings → Privacy → Data Rights
- **Mail**: CallWall Privacy Team, [Your Address]

We will respond within 30 days of verification.
